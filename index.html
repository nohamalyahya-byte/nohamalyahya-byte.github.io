Selected projects

Case studies

Patient Experience Digital Platform

Digital transformation · Patient experience

Challenge
Patient Experience activities and follow-up processes were distributed across different workflows, creating a need for a centralized digital platform that could improve visibility, accountability, and coordination.
My role
Contributed to defining and analyzing operational requirements, reviewing proposed platform features and workflows, coordinating with relevant stakeholders and IT, and following up on feedback, development, and required enhancements.
Impact
Established a structured digital framework for Patient Experience operations, supporting more organized follow-up, clearer responsibilities, and a more integrated approach to managing related activities.

Executive Operations Management Platform

Digital transformation · Executive operations

Challenge
Executive tasks, requests, approvals, and follow-up activities were managed through different channels, making it difficult to maintain clear visibility over responsibilities, status, and completion.
My role
Contributed to designing the platform’s operating model, analyzing business needs, defining workflows for tasks, requests, and approvals, and specifying roles, permissions, notification requirements, and reporting needs. Also supported review and follow-up of platform development and enhancements.
Impact
Designed a centralized model for managing executive operations, aimed at improving visibility, accountability, and follow-up across departments while giving management a clearer view of ongoing work.

Physician Document Submission & Verification Portal

Digital transformation · Process improvement

Challenge
Physician documents and credentialing requirements involved multiple stakeholders and review steps, creating a need for a more structured and traceable process for submission, verification, and approval.
My role
Contributed to defining portal requirements, mandatory documentation, workflow stages, roles and permissions, and review and approval paths. Also supported data organization and follow-up on physician file completion and quality.
Impact
Supported the development of a more structured and traceable digital process for managing physician documentation and verification across a scope of 157 physicians.

HIS Enhancement & System Improvement

Health IT · Operational improvement

Challenge
Following the implementation of the Hospital Information System, operational and technical issues emerged across departments, requiring a structured approach to capture, analyze, prioritize, and follow up on improvement needs.
My role
Collected, classified, and analyzed system-related requests and issues, identified operational gaps and improvement opportunities, prepared executive reports, and coordinated follow-up with IT on required enhancements and corrective actions.
Impact
Created a consolidated view of HIS-related issues and improvement requirements, turning scattered operational feedback into structured, trackable needs that support system efficiency, usability, and operational continuity.

Healthcare Operations & Performance Improvement

Operations · Performance improvement

Challenge
Operational data and service-related observations across several healthcare functions needed to be translated into clear insights that could help identify gaps, understand performance issues, and support improvement decisions.
My role
Analyzed operational data, KPIs, and workflows across areas including Outpatient, Emergency, Inpatient, Medical Approvals, and Patient Experience. Developed executive reports and analytical dashboards, identified improvement opportunities, and coordinated follow-up with relevant departments.
Impact
Transformed operational data into actionable management insights, supporting performance monitoring, prioritization of improvement areas, and more data-informed operational decision-making.
